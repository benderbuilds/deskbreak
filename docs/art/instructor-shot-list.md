## Rules for every frame

- Square, at least 1024 px, no text of any kind. Import with `node scripts/import-art-frames.mjs <stem>` after adding a recipe to `design/instructor/frames.json`; it writes WebP and rebuilds the manifest.
- Start (`<stem>.webp`) and end (`<stem>-b.webp`) share camera, lens, light, instructor and props. Only the body moves.
- Each-side moves are shot once, exactly as the cue describes; that frame is the Left side step. The player mirrors it for the Right side step and gives both sides time and a switch cue.
- Close-framed moves that are the same seated or standing get one shot; the resolver uses it for both setups.
- Floor moves are shot on the mat and only appear for people who opted in to floor work.
- Keep the SVG until the photo has been checked on a preview. A photo that fails to load falls back to it.

Angle: side, full. The avoid (sagging hips) is only visible as a broken head-to-heel line from the side. Full body so heels and head are both in frame. The desk must read as solid and fixed: oak legs, no casters, per the cue.

## All other moves

Prompts for every frame are in `design/instructor/shot-list.json`. The angle reasoning:

- **Chin tuck** (seated): side, close. The fault (nodding instead of gliding) is a sagittal movement; profile shows the glide.
- **Chin tuck hold** (seated): side, close. Same plane as chin tuck; the hold is the end frame.
- **Suboccipital nod** (seated): side, close. A small nod is only visible in profile; close framing keeps it readable.
- **Neck side stretch** (seated): front, medium. The fault (turning the chin as you tip) shows from the front.
- **Neck side stretch** (standing): front, medium. The fault (turning the chin as you tip) shows from the front.
- **Neck rotation** (seated): front, close. Rotation reads from the front; overhead would hide the face.
- **Upper trap and levator release** (seated): front, medium. The fault (shrugging the stretching shoulder) shows from the front.
- **Shoulder rolls** (seated): side, medium. The backward roll is a sagittal arc; profile shows lift, back, drop.
- **Shoulder rolls** (standing): side, medium. The backward roll is a sagittal arc; profile shows lift, back, drop.
- **Unshrug** (seated): front, medium. Shoulder height is a frontal-plane cue.
- **Unshrug** (standing): front, medium. Shoulder height is a frontal-plane cue.
- **Seated scap squeeze** (seated): three-quarter, medium. Shrugging shows from the front, the squeeze from behind; three-quarter rear shows both.
- **Scap pack hold** (seated): three-quarter, medium. As above: three-quarter rear shows the blades and the shoulder height.
- **Scap pack hold** (standing): three-quarter, medium. As above: three-quarter rear shows the blades and the shoulder height.
- **Doorway or wall chest opener** (standing): three-quarter, full. The elbow height and the step both need to read; three-quarter from the open side.
- **Chair open chest** (seated): side, medium. The fault (flaring ribs, overarching) is sagittal.
- **Elbows-pinned external rotation + scap squeeze** (seated): front, medium. Elbow flare and shrug both show from the front.
- **Elbows-pinned external rotation + scap squeeze** (standing): front, medium. Elbow flare and shrug both show from the front.
- **Wall angels** (standing): front, full. Arm path reads from the front; the low-back fault is noted in the cue.
- **Thoracic extension over chair** (seated): side, medium. Extension is sagittal; profile shows where it bends.
- **Seated thoracic rotation** (seated): front, medium. Hips staying square against a turned trunk only reads from the front.
- **Seated side bend** (seated): front, medium. Side bending is frontal-plane.
- **Seated pelvic tilts** (seated): side, medium. Pelvic tilt is sagittal and small; profile at seat height.
- **Seated lumbar support reset** (seated): side, medium. Spinal curve only reads in profile.
- **Seated figure-4** (seated): front, full. The fault (pressing the knee down) and the cross both read from the front.
- **Seated hip opener** (seated): front, full. Knee path is frontal.
- **Seated hip windshield wipers** (seated): front, full. Sweep is frontal.
- **Standing hip flexor stretch** (standing): side, full. The fault (arching the low back) is sagittal.
- **Standing hip hinge (desk)** (standing): side, full. Rounding versus hinging only reads in profile.
- **Standing glute squeeze** (standing): three-quarter, full. Locked knees read from the side, the squeeze from behind; three-quarter rear.
- **Sit-to-stand + glute squeeze** (standing): three-quarter, full. Knee cave shows from the front, momentum from the side; three-quarter front.
- **Hamstring hinge (desk-assisted)** (standing): side, full. Rounding the low back is sagittal.
- **Wrist flexor stretch** (seated): side, close. The bend at the wrist and the soft elbow read in profile.
- **Wrist extensor stretch** (seated): side, close. As above.
- **Finger fans** (seated): front, close. Spread versus fist is frontal; hands only.
- **Standing wrist shake-out** (standing): front, medium. The fault (shoulders up by the ears) is frontal.
- **Stand tall and breathe** (standing): side, full. Locked knees show in profile.
- **Standing overhead reach** (standing): side, full. Rib flare is sagittal.
- **Seated march** (seated): front, full. Matches the reference sheet: the knee lift and upright trunk read from the front.
- **Standing quad stretch** (standing): side, full. Knees together and the bent leg read best in profile; the cue covers rotation.
- **Ankle pumps** (seated): side, close. Ankle motion is sagittal; framed on lower legs and feet.
- **Foot tripod + toe spread** (standing): overhead-three-quarter, close. Toe spread reads from above and in front; framed on the feet only. Bare feet for this move only.
- **Long exhale reset** (seated): three-quarter, medium. Breath shows as shoulder and chest change; three-quarter keeps the face readable.
- **Long exhale reset** (standing): three-quarter, medium. Breath shows as shoulder and chest change; three-quarter keeps the face readable.
- **Paced breathing** (seated): three-quarter, medium. As above.
- **Paced breathing** (standing): three-quarter, medium. As above.
- **Sit bones find** (seated): side, full. Perching versus balanced is sagittal.
- **Desk wiggle reset** (seated): front, medium. A micro movement reads in the frontal plane.
- **Screen distance + blink** (seated): three-quarter, medium. Shows the gaze change from screen to far point.
- **Screen distance + blink** (standing): three-quarter, medium. Shows the gaze change from screen to far point.
- **Short walk** (standing): three-quarter, full. Walking reads in three-quarter.
- **Stand and shift** (standing): front, full. Weight shift is frontal.
